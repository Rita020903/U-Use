## Invitation

We are studying how students access items for occasional needs. This voluntary session takes about 15 minutes. You may skip any question or stop at any time. We request no student ID, payment information, precise location or complete timetable. We would like to keep anonymised notes. Recording requires separate permission. We will explain the agreed retention period before collecting data. Participation has no effect on your grades.

## Recent Incident

1. What item did you last need temporarily? When and for how long?
2. What did you try first, and what happened?
3. What time or money did the process cost? What did you do instead?
4. What made an arrangement acceptable or unacceptable?
5. As an owner, what terms would you need before making an item available?
