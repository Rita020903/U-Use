## 0:00–0:35 / 郭宇璇 (Rita) / Venture recap

**Picture:** Presenter, U Use identity, then a dated request. Caption: “Working prototype. Synthetic demonstration account.”

Hello, we are U Use Team, our working team name. U Use helps students access an item for the dates they need it. A planned project or event can require equipment that a student does not want to buy. Finding the item still leaves dates, handoff and return to arrange. We support buying, borrowing, renting and permanent swaps, starting with a proposed pilot across eight DTS classes.

## 0:35–1:10 / 毛相宜 / Problem and target customer

**Picture:** Three qualitative insights with anonymised IDs, then the optional map and reviewed timetable. Caption: “15 team-provided interview summaries. Qualitative evidence.”

The eight classes contain approximately 430 students, according to our team estimate. That is a population boundary, not measured demand. Our fifteen team-provided interview summaries include people who prefer friends or existing services and people who would not use another platform. We therefore focus on a planned need without a suitable item from a known peer. Location and timetable import are optional. Suggestions still require explicit agreement.

## 1:10–2:00 / 黄俊杰 / Competitors and strategy

**Picture:** Three named substitutes, their source dates and a five-row comparison of tasks. Do not use unsupported “competitor has no feature” crosses.

Students already have strong alternatives. Xianyu offers broad resale listings. Renrenzu offers equipment rental, and ATRenew combines trade-in and resale services. Friends and class groups can be simpler for an occasional need. We compare five tasks: a request with dates, mutual free-time suggestions, explicit handoff and return, control over disclosure, and campus operating support. These describe our implemented workflow, not five exclusive advantages. Competitors can copy features. Our strategy is to test whether relevant local supply and accountable coordination make a specific task easier. We must benchmark that benefit before calling it a durable advantage.

## 2:00–2:40 / 李元龙 / Demonstrated progress

**Picture:** Actual product flow with synthetic accounts, QA dates, then a blank/real-status evidence register. Label engineering checks separately from participant observations.

The working prototype now includes private accounts, date checks, a needs board and bilateral handoff and return records. Each person can import a timetable image or PDF, review classes and request compatible time suggestions. The algorithm checks calendars, availability and existing bookings. Engineering acceptance on 18 and 19 October used isolated data and test accounts. It is not evidence of customer adoption. Real task observations must establish whether students understand the fees, can use their own timetables and can proceed without sharing location.

## 2:40–3:40 / 汪睿 / Financial forecast

**Picture:** Financial model, Case selected = Base. Display “Six-month planning case, not observed results.” Show completed requests, fee revenue, cash costs and break-even threshold.

Our six-month base case assumes 38 completed requests, not 38 distinct users. Half are fee-bearing, with twenty yuan of consideration per paid request. The pilot fee is zero. A hypothetical five-percent fee starts in month four. That produces thirteen yuan of platform revenue against 687 yuan and twenty fen of planned cash costs, leaving a cash shortfall of 674 yuan and twenty fen. Team time is separate. Later, the contribution is only thirty fen per completed request. Covering one hundred yuan of monthly fixed costs would require 334 completions. That is a mathematical threshold, not a credible demand forecast. We must validate pricing or reduce operating costs before claiming profitability.

## 3:40–4:30 / 汤鋆秋 / Roadmap and decision gates

**Picture:** Dated roadmap with owners, dependencies and status. Distinguish completed engineering work from planned user evidence and future deployment.

After A1, we plan to observe at least three real users, record failures and retest changes. Each participant should use their own timetable or choose manual entry. The next gate is relevant supply and genuine completed requests, with no severe privacy or loss incident. Permission to recruit, willing owners and safe operations are dependencies. We freeze the video internally on 16 December and aim to upload on the seventeenth. If demand, supply or operating effort disappoints, we will simplify to a manual bulletin. Public launch also requires live email, real devices, production infrastructure and an accountable moderator.

## 4:30–5:25 / 黄禹轩 / Funding proposition and close

**Picture:** CNY900 proposed request. Show CNY200 first release and CNY700 conditional release. Uses: fixed cash budget CNY680, variable cash CNY7.20, reserve CNY212.80. Label “Proposed, subject to approval.”

We propose a capped, non-dilutive validation request of nine hundred yuan. Two hundred supports the first pilot. A further seven hundred would require a documented review and approval. The allocation is 680 yuan of fixed cash budget, seven yuan and twenty fen of variable cash and 212 yuan and eighty fen of reserve. No funder or partnership is committed. This base case does not support an equity valuation or a promise of financial returns. Support would fund a limited test of willingness to pay, reliable supply and operating cost. We will reconsider investment only if those results support a viable model. Otherwise, keeping the service small is a valid decision. Thank you.
