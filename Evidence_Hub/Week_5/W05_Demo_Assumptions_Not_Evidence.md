## 3. Conservative Pilot Funnel

| 漏斗 | 演示数值 | 备注 |
|---|---:|---|
| 可触达学生 | 300 | 只算课程群、熟人群、一个社团群 |
| 点击或打开原型 | 60 | 20% 打开率假设 |
| 完成问卷或任务 | 24 | 对应上方 synthetic scenario |
| 愿意留下联系方式继续测试 | 8 | 仅作为内测池 |
| 可能发生真实试用 | 0-3 | 课程阶段不承诺真实交易 |

## 4. How To Phrase It

可以写：

> Based on a conservative demo scenario, the pilot would be considered promising if around 20-30 students complete the survey and at least half of them prefer borrowing or renting for 1-7 day use cases. This is a planning assumption, not collected evidence yet.

不要写：

> We surveyed 24 students and proved 71% have demand.
