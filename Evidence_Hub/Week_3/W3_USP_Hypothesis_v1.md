# W3_USP Hypothesis v1：U Use

课程依据：ENT303TC Week 3 —— draft USP hypothesis，写成**一个可测试的主张**。
验收标准：必须是下面这个句式的完整填空，六个空都要有具体内容，且可以被验证。

## 1. 课程要求的句式（必须照抄结构）

```text
For [target user] who currently [pain / workaround],
[venture name] is a [category] that [core benefit],
unlike [named competitor], which [key limitation].
```

要求：
- `[named competitor]` 必须是**点名的一个具体竞品或替代方案**，不能写 "other platforms"。
- `[key limitation]` 要是这个竞品**真实存在**的局限，不能是编的。
- `[core benefit]` 要能被证据或测试验证。

## 2. v1 定稿（组内选一个，其余作为备选）

**版本 A（推荐，以闲鱼为对手）**

> For XJTLU students who currently buy things they only need for a few days, or post a message in WeChat groups and hope someone replies, U Use is a verified campus item-access platform that lets them buy, borrow, rent or swap a specific item for a chosen time window, with deposit, return confirmation and a public handoff point, unlike Xianyu, which is built for second-hand trading and leaves delivery, deposit and return arrangements to private chat.

**版本 B（以微信群为对手，更贴学生现状）**

> For XJTLU students who currently rely on WeChat groups to borrow things from classmates, U Use is a verified campus item-access platform that makes items searchable by campus, availability and access type, with deposit rules and return confirmation built in, unlike WeChat groups, which are a rolling message feed with no structure and no way to settle damage or late return.

**版本 C（以设备租赁店为对手，商业对比更强）**

> For XJTLU students who need equipment for one exam, one presentation or one event, U Use is a peer-to-peer campus item-access platform that covers everyday long-tail items at near-zero cost, unlike commercial rental shops, which charge by the day but only stock a narrow range of high-value equipment and are not built around campus handoff points.

## 4. 怎么证明这个 USP 是「可测试的」（Week 3 要求的 testable claim）

把它转成可以被证伪的测试：

| 测试 | 方法 | 通过标准 | 时间 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 用户能否复述区别 | 看完首页 30 秒后问「它和闲鱼有什么不同」 | 5 人中至少 4 人能说出「按时间借用」「有形规则」两点 | Week 5 |
| Pain 是否真实 | 问卷 Q3 + 访谈追问最近一次经历 | ≥60% 受访者过去一学期有过该场景 | Week 4-5 |
| 核心机制是否被接受 | 原型测试：押金 + 归还确认 + 公共交付点 | ≥70% 表示押金机制可接受 | Week 5 |
| 是否有切换理由 | 问「你会优先用哪个渠道，为什么」 | 至少 3 人说出比微信群更清楚的原因 | Week 5 |

**如果测试没通过，USP 要改，不是把数据藏起来。** 这是课程明确要求的 pivot/persevere 记录。

## 5. 不要用的写法（课程会扣分）

- ✗ "U Use is a better campus second-hand platform."（在比较形容词上打转，不可测试）
- ✗ "U Use is the leading platform for XJTLU students."（没有对手、没有局限）
- ✗ 一段没有句式的描述性段落（v0 草稿里的写法）。
- ✗ 把「环保 / 回收 / 可持续」写进核心 benefit（会和回收方向的小组重叠，也偏离定位）。
